Research

Silent or Wrong: Producer-Contract Defects in Shipped Prometheus Alerting Rules

Alerting rules ship with exporters, Helm charts and monitoring mixins to people who did not write them. Many quietly depend on facts about their metrics, such as units, types, labels and histogram buckets, that the rule never states. When those facts are wrong, the alert never fires or fires on the wrong condition. I call these unstated facts the rule's producer contract, and the bugs they cause producer-contract defects. The study examines how often this happens in widely used open-source rules, where it comes from and whether current tools catch it.

I chose the topic, designed the study and ran it with LLM agents under my supervision.
