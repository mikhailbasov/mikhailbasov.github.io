Research

Silent or Wrong: Producer-Contract Defects in Shipped Prometheus Alerting Rules

Alerting rules ship with exporters, Helm charts and monitoring mixins to people who did not write them. A rule often depends on facts about its metrics that it never states: units, types, labels, histogram buckets. When such a fact is wrong, the alert never fires or fires on the wrong condition.

I audited 1,808 shipped rules from 86 rule sets and confirmed 31 distinct defects. In at least 19 of them the alert cannot fire in the situation it was written for. promtool and Pint in offline mode find none of the 31.

I chose the topic, designed the study and ran it with LLM agents under my supervision.

Submitted to arXiv (cs.SE) in November 2026. The link will appear here when the preprint is announced.
